# Rarelift privacy policy

*Last updated: 29 October 2026*

Rarelift is a workout tracker made by Harshit Jani. This policy explains what happens to your data.
The short version: **your training data stays on your phone. We don't collect it, see it, or sell it.**

## What the app stores
Everything you enter (exercises, workouts, sets, notes, body weight and settings) is saved only in the
app's private storage on your device. There are no accounts, no cloud sync, no analytics and no ads.
Nothing you log is sent to us or to anyone else.

## When data leaves your phone
Only when you choose to send it:
- **Backup / CSV export / share image**: the file goes wherever you pick (a folder, Drive, a chat).
- **Send feedback / share crash log**: opens your own email or messaging app with the text visible
  before you send it. The crash log is a technical error report kept on your phone until you share
  or clear it.

## Purchases (Google Play edition)
"Rarelift Pro" is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment under
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app only asks Google Play whether
this account owns Pro; it never sees your payment details.

## Updates (GitHub edition only)
The edition downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub's public releases page for new versions. That request
is made to GitHub (see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement))
and carries no personal data from the app.

## Permissions
- **Notifications**: the rest timer countdown and the "rest is over" alert.
- **Vibration**: the rest-over buzz.
- **Internet**: Google Play purchases (Play edition) or update checks (GitHub edition).
- **Install other apps** (GitHub edition only): to install its own updates when you tap Install.

## Deleting your data
Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. You can also delete individual workouts in History.

## Children
Rarelift is not directed at children under 13.
