## Rarelift 2.1

- **Rest alerts that actually arrive.** On Android 12 and newer, the "rest is over" buzz could fail to
  fire while the phone was locked. Fixed.
- **Reorder exercises** in a workout preview and in your templates.
- **Plate calculator**: tap the calculator icon on any exercise to see what to load on each side.
- **Tap or drag on charts** to see the exact numbers for any session; points are spaced by real dates.
- **Past workouts** you log now show the targets and PRs as they were on that day.
