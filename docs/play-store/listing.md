**Full description** (4000 max)
```
Most workout apps reward doing more. Rarelift is built for doing less, harder.

It's a training log for high-intensity training (HIT): one all-out set per exercise, taken to
failure, then enough rest to actually recover and grow. Rarelift does the bookkeeping so you can
put everything into the set.

KNOW WHAT TO BEAT
• Every exercise shows a clear target from your last session: add weight, beat it by a rep, or build reps
• Double progression with your own rep range and weight jump per exercise
• Last time, personal records and your notes are right there while you train

TRAIN ONE EXERCISE AT A TIME
• Preview a workout, tap Start, then a focused screen for each exercise
• Big weight and reps fields with +/− steppers, made for sweaty hands
• Mark a set to failure and log what came after: forced reps, negatives, rest-pause, static holds
• Pre-exhaust supersets: link an isolation move to a compound and the app tells you to skip the rest
• A rest timer that keeps running with your phone locked

RECOVER ON PURPOSE
• See which workouts you've recovered from, and get the next one suggested
• Stall detection: when a lift stops moving, Rarelift suggests more rest or a back-off, not more sets
• A weekly strip and a calendar of your training

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your phone
• Back up and restore to a file, export to CSV, log past workouts
• Works in kg or lbs

RARELIFT PRO (one-time purchase, no subscription)
• Stats: consistency over 12 weeks, strength trend for every lift, body-weight log
• Ready-made routines with pre-exhaust supersets already linked
• Share any workout as an image

Train hard. Rest properly. Lift rarely.

Rarelift is a training log, not medical advice. Talk to a professional before starting a new
training programme.
```
